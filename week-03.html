Week 3: Risers, Fallers, and a Whole Lot of Faith

Week 3 is in the books, friends, and the trainer's table was the busiest spot in the league. Lineups got shuffled, a few teams found another gear, and a couple of managers are learning that patience is a fruit of the Spirit — and also a fantasy strategy. Let's tip it off.

1. Triton Aggies even

Still perched at No. 1, still 3-0, still raining threes and swishing freebies. Jung's crew is giving away a little field-goal efficiency most weeks, but when you shoot it like that from deep, nobody's complaining. Hold the throne.

2. God's Favorite Danny up 4

Big jump from sixth to second, and Danny's bunch did it 3-0 while navigating a crowded injury report — Jimmy Butler III and Shaedon Sharpe both parked on the IL. Climbing while shorthanded probably says something good about the depth here. Elite assists, elite blocks; the turnovers are the one thing to tidy up. God's Favorite Danny, indeed.
