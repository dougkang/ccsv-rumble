Draft Night at the Rock: 180 Picks, Zero Humility, Plenty of Snacks

Eighteen teams, ten rounds, one hundred and eighty picks, and somehow the only thing nobody fought over was the parking lot. The top of round one went about as chalky as a church gym floor: Jokić first, Wembanyama second, and then a full-on guard run with Luka, Shai and Cade going third, fourth and fifth before anyone could say "wait, I needed a point guard." Big men ruled the back half of the first — LeBron sixth, Giannis seventh, Şengün fourteenth — and the rookie of the night was Cooper Flagg at fifteenth, which is either a steal or a leap of faith, and in this league those are the same thing. The real gasp came at the turn. Stephen Curry lasted until the nineteenth pick and Kevin Durant until the twentieth, which means two living legends got drafted after the snacks ran out. Joel Embiid went fifty-fifth, Ja Morant eighty-fifth, and Zion Williamson ninety-second — the bargain bin had some very large names in it. Centres came off the board in waves all night, and by round eight there was a genuine stampede for anything over seven feet. If your roster has four guards and a prayer, you know who you are.

1. Woo-Tang 4eva new

Woo-Tang 4eva turned the back of round one into a bank heist: Jalen Brunson and Stephen Curry back to back at the eighteenth and nineteenth, then AJ Dybantsa and Joel Embiid at the next turn. Cash Rules Everything Around Me — and so do threes, with Ty Jerome, Dillon Brooks and Alex Caruso filling in around a frontcourt that leans hard on Embiid and Porziņģis staying upright. Grade: A.

2. Elsie’s World new

Elsie's World went party time, excellent — Shai at fourth, then Evan Mobley and Jaren Jackson Jr. to build a frontcourt that looks like a locked door. The back half is a nice mix of shooting and connective tissue in Knueppel, Pritchard and Braun, though after Shai the guard room is more role players than rainmakers. Grade: A.

3. The Wongster new

The Wongster bet on the future and then hedged with Kyrie: Cooper Flagg at fifteenth and Scottie Barnes at twenty-second is a do-everything forward duo, and the Dallas stack is very on brand. Okongwu, Randle and Wendell Carter Jr. give them real size; the guard depth behind Kyrie and Coby White is where the squinting starts. Grade: A-.

4. WEE-TOO-TALL new

WEE-TOO-TALL read their own team name and took it as a shopping list — LeBron, Anthony Davis, Siakam, Sharpe, Claxton, Gafford and Diabaté. That is a frontcourt you could build a sanctuary on, but with Herro, Harper and Fears handling the ball, they'll need assists and threes from somewhere or the tall jokes write themselves. Grade: B+.

5. Triton Aggies new

Triton Aggies took Luka third and then played it beautifully straight — Bam, Brandon Miller, Alexander-Walker, Josh Hart and Herbert Jones is a roster with a job description for everybody. Zach Edey in round five gives them rebounds in bulk; the question is whether the supporting scoring shows up when Luka rests. Grade: A-.

6. 🥭 smoothie’s team new

🥭 smoothie's team put the best ingredient in the blender first with Jokić at one overall, then doubled up at the turn with Jalen Duren and James Harden. Naz Reid, Ausar Thompson and Jalen Suggs add defence and juice, but it's a fruit-forward recipe — a lot rides on Harden still conducting the offence. Grade: A-.

7. Keyboard Cats new

Keyboard Cats mashed Ctrl+C on shot-blockers: Giannis at seventh, then Walker Kessler and Alex Sarr to go with Booker's buckets. Nembhard, VanVleet and Anthony Black keep the assists coming; three big men who thrive in the paint means the three-point column may need a copy-paste of its own. Grade: B+.

8. The best wongster new

The best wongster grabbed Karl-Anthony Towns and Trae Young at the turn, which is a lovely pairing of a stretch big and a man who has never met a pass he didn't like. Then came Zubac in round four and Poeltl in round seven, so that's three centres on one roster — a choice, but a very rebound-rich one. Grade: B+.

9. Peter Piper Picked a Pick&Roll new

Peter Piper picked a peck of perimeter defenders: Anthony Edwards and Chet Holmgren up top, then Derrick White, Gobert, Jrue Holiday, Tari Eason and Devin Vassell. If stocks and blocks win weeks, this team is a problem; if they need volume scoring beyond Ant, the pick-and-roll had better roll. Grade: A-.

10. pau-erful slacker new

pau-erful slacker was anything but slack early — Tatum ninth, Jamal Murray, Jalen Williams, then Cameron Boozer, Paul George and Brandon Ingram for a wing rotation three deep in every direction. It's a roster betting on health and return-to-form stories, and only Isaiah Jackson really protects the rim. Grade: B+.

11. The Lou Whoppers new

The Lou Whoppers ordered it with extra beef: Wembanyama second, then Markkanen, Sabonis, Hartenstein, Kuminga and Jerami Grant stacked like a double patty. Rebounds and field goal percentage should be a feast, but with Fox, Rollins and Tre Jones carrying the backcourt, they'll need the guards to hold the pickles and the turnovers. Grade: B+.

12. California Burritos new

California Burritos wrapped everything in the tortilla — Jalen Johnson, Amen Thompson, Paolo Banchero, Jarrett Allen, Jabari Smith Jr., Maluach, Nurkić and Raynaud. That's a monster rebounding build with Garland for assists; the three-point line is the one ingredient they left off the order. Grade: B.

13. #14th Scariest Team new

#14th Scariest Team paired Donovan Mitchell and Kevin Durant at the turn, which is a frankly unscary amount of scoring for a team with that name. Clingan, Zion and Myles Turner give them size with upside, and Mikal Bridges and Nesmith keep the lights on — now the boom-or-bust bigs have to actually boom. Grade: B+.

14. Lebron’s Coach new

Lebron's Coach drafted an entire roster and no LeBron, which is the boldest coaching decision of the night. Maxey, Trey Murphy III, Bane and Michael Porter Jr. is a shooting clinic, and Draymond and Murray-Boyles bring defence — but the centre spot is more of a suggestion than a plan. Grade: B.

15. Cade and friends new

Cade and friends delivered exactly what the label promised, taking Cade Cunningham fifth and then assembling the friends: Giddey, Kawhi, Dejounte Murray, Ware, Collins, Missi and Vučević. Truth in advertising is rare and beautiful; the frontcourt is deep enough that minutes may get crowded. Grade: B.

16. Isaac Destroyer of Worlds new

Isaac Destroyer of Worlds went full chaos: LaMelo, Jaylen Brown, LaVine, Ja Morant, Ben Simmons, Klay and Tim Hardaway Jr. If the shooting and the availability hold, this thing scores from everywhere — but with Jaden McDaniels and Peyton Watson as the tallest men in the building, the paint is wide open for visitors. Grade: C+.

17. Willy the baller new

Willy the baller went guard-first and kept dribbling: Haliburton at eleventh, then Avdija, Franz Wagner, Stephon Castle, Jalen Green and Jared McCain. It's a fun, assist-heavy build with Mitchell Robinson and Bassey holding down the glass, and a lot depends on the eleventh pick suiting up. Grade: C+.

18. God's Favorite Danny new

God's Favorite Danny was clearly blessed with a steals mandate — Dyson Daniels, Davion Mitchell and Amen-adjacent pressure everywhere, built around Şengün and Austin Reaves. Lillard, Quickley and Jimmy Butler III add veteran scoring, but outside Şengün and Jalen Smith the rebounding may require divine intervention. Grade: C+.

So that's the board. Eighteen rosters, eighteen theories, and at least four teams convinced they solved centre by drafting all of them. Week one is when the group chat earns its keep. Watch the rotations settle, watch who's streaming the waiver wire before Sunday service, and watch whoever took three big men learn the meaning of "minutes." Nobody's won anything yet and nobody's lost anything either — which is, honestly, the best week of the year. Set your lineups. Check your bench. Be nice to each other, at least until tip-off. Overall draft grade: A-. Deep, chaotic, occasionally inspired, and only mildly centre-obsessed. Well done, everybody.
