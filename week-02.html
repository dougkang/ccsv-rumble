Week 2: The Last Shall Be First, and the First Shall Be Tenth

Week 2 shook the standings like an usher shaking a collection plate. Four teams climbed four spots or more, two fell nine, and the injury report read longer than the announcements. Nobody panic — it's early, the season is long, and grace abounds. Here's where everyone sits this morning.

1. Triton Aggies up 4

1. Triton Aggies (Jung) — up 4 to the top of the mountain, 2-0 and stealing everything that isn't bolted down. Honestly, no obvious culprit for the climb; their week was quiet off the floor, which is its own kind of blessing. The assists keep wandering off, but when you shoot free throws like that, you can live with it. Aggie-nize over the details later, Jung.

2. California Burritos up 2

2. California Burritos (Brian) — 2-0 and wrapped up tight at No. 2. Great from the line, great from the field; a little loose with steals and threes. Everything's in the tortilla, nothing's falling out.

3. Elsie’s World down 1

3. Elsie's World (Daniel) — a one-spot slide that's really no slide at all. The 1-1 record undersells it, and the efficiency remains a joy to watch. We're all just living in Elsie's World.

4. pau-erful slacker up 6

4. pau-erful slacker (pa) — up 6 despite an 0-2 record, which is the week's great paradox. If anything, the climb came the hard way: Jalen Williams, Brandon Ingram and Cason Wallace all showed up on the out list, and pa kept dishing anyway. The schedule has been unkind. The team has not been bad. Pau-se and appreciate it.

5. Lebron’s Coach up 4

5. Lebron's Coach (Noah) — up 4, 2-0, and raining threes. There's no obvious culprit in the news for the jump; sometimes the ark just floats, Noah. Take care of the ball and this stays fun.

6. God's Favorite Danny up 6

6. God's Favorite Danny (Danny) — up 6 and unbeaten, and it likely reflects a roster that kept humming while Jimmy Butler III and Shaedon Sharpe sat on the shelf. Assists and steals in bunches. The free throws may need a prayer meeting, but we're not complaining at 2-0.

7. The Wongster up 11

7. The Wongster (Ethan) — the week's biggest riser, up 11 spots to seventh. And here's the fun part: there's no obvious culprit for it in the news, unless you count Coby White and Draymond Green being unavailable, which should have hurt, not helped. Blocks and steals everywhere. Two Wongsters don't make a right, but this one's doing just fine.

8. Keyboard Cats even

8. Keyboard Cats (Doug Kang) — held steady at eighth with an 0-2 record that feels deeply unfair. The boards and the blocks are real. The wins will come.

9. The Lou Whoppers down 3

9. The Lou Whoppers (Paul) — slipped three spots while going 2-0, which is a sentence that will annoy Paul all week. No obvious culprit here; the news was quiet on their end. Have it your way, the record still says undefeated.

10. Woo-Tang 4eva down 9

10. Woo-Tang 4eva (Woo) — from first to tenth, the hardest fall of the week. It probably didn't help that Stephen Curry and Alex Caruso both landed on the out list. Still elite at scoring and at the line. Woo-Tang is still 4eva; this is just a tough chapter.

11. 🥭 smoothie’s team up 2

12. WEE-TOO-TALL down 9

13. #14th Scariest Team up 3

13. #14th Scariest Team (Paul) — up three despite an 0-2 week, and there's no obvious explanation in the news for the bump. Efficient shooting and plenty of blocks. Now the 13th scariest, which means the name needs an edit.

That's Week 2 — a lot of movement, a lot of ice packs, and a reminder that the standings this early are written in pencil, not stone. Be kind in the group chat, set your lineups, and we'll do it all again next Sunday.
