Pass the Gravy, Carve the Standings: Week 5 Thanksgiving Power Rankings

Happy Thanksgiving, fam. The oven's on, the table's set, and somebody's cousin is already arguing about whose mashed potatoes are better. Week 5 gave us trades, IL shuffles, a few folks limping back into the lineup, and one team that slid down the table like a dinner roll off a tilted plate. Grab a plate, go back for seconds, and let's see who's stuffed and who's still waiting on the turkey.

1. God's Favorite Danny even

Still the big bird at the head of the table. 5-0, buckets everywhere, threes raining down like cranberry sauce. The turnovers are the one side dish nobody asked for, but when you're undefeated you get to pick the seating chart. Danny also waved goodbye to DeMar DeRozan this week — leftovers happen.

2. The Wongster up 4

Up four spots to second, and the 2-3 record does not do this plate justice — Ethan's been getting the schedule's smallest slice of pie. Scottie Barnes coming off IL and Josh Giddey arriving via trade (with Kyrie headed the other way) may have something to do with the glow-up, plus a little roster housekeeping. Assists and scoring are flowing; the ball security is the gravy boat that keeps tipping over.

3. 3rd Rock from the Suns down 1

A one-spot nudge, which around here is basically the table wobbling. Doug's blocks and ball security are elite, the record just hasn't matched the cooking. Giannis got listed out while Nembhard and VanVleet came back — an eventful week in the kitchen.

4. The Lou Whoppers up 3

Up three and sitting pretty at 4-1. Getting De'Aaron Fox back off the out list likely didn't hurt one bit. Free throws and rebounds are the Whoppers' bread and butter — or should we say their stuffing and yams.

5. Triton Aggies down 2

A gentle two-step back, nothing to lose sleep over. Jung stayed busy, adding Tre Jones, parting with Reed Sheppard, and sending CJ McCollum to IL. Those free throws remain money.

6. 🥭 smoothie’s team up 4

Four spots up the table and smelling good. Jalen Suggs returning from IL may be part of the aroma, even with P.J. Washington getting listed out. Assists and boards are plentiful; the free throws could use a little basting.

7. Lebron’s Coach down 3

Down three despite a shiny 4-1. Noah had a whirlwind week — Desmond Bane traded out for Moussa Diabaté, Jerami Grant moving on, Grayson Allen listed out — and any of that may have left the plate looking a little rearranged. The good news: Michael Porter Jr. and Trey Murphy III are back off IL, and Shaedon Sharpe joined the party. Blocks are still swatting away.

8. Woo-Tang 4eva up 1

A quiet one-spot step up for Woo. Steph went to IL, but Embiid and Ty Jerome are off the out list, so the table's filling back in. Scoring and free throws remain a tasty combo.

9. pau-erful slacker up 3

Up three spots and reaching across the table for more. Brandon Ingram no longer being listed out probably helped the plate look fuller, even with Paul George and Cameron Boozer going out. Steals and free throws are the specialty; the field-goal efficiency is still a bit undercooked.

10. Elsie’s World down 2

A two-spot slide, which is within the normal wobble of the folding table. Daniel's week was a crowded infirmary — Knueppel, Mobley and Jaren Jackson Jr. all to IL, SGA listed out. The threes and scoring are still there. Hang in there, Elsie's World.

11. California Burritos down 6

Down six, the biggest drop of the week, and honestly? No obvious culprit here — the Burritos had a completely quiet week on the wire. The 3-2 record is intact and the blocks are still coming. Sometimes the plate just gets bumped on the way to the table. Brian will wrap it back up.

12. The best wongster up 1

A one-spot bump for Keenan, who's quietly 3-1-1 and unbeaten in the loss column more often than not. OG Anunoby and RJ Barrett both landed on the out list and Norman Powell went from IL to out — that's a thin carving knife. Boards and blocks keep the plate full.

13. WEE-TOO-TALL down 2

Still waiting on that first win, and the schedule has not exactly been passing the rolls. The good news: Desmond Bane arrived via trade, and the field-goal efficiency and ball security are genuinely top shelf. Dylan Harper and Nic Claxton getting listed out made for a thin week. The W is coming, Jonnel — keep setting a place for it.

14. Peter Piper Picked a Pick&Roll even

Peter Piper picked a steady spot and stayed in it. 3-2 with steals and rebounds in abundance — a quiet, tidy week.

15. Cade and friends up 2

Two spots up for Ling-jet, who had a busy week with Kyrie Irving arriving, Josh Giddey departing, and Jerami Grant joining the roster. The blocks and ball security are already strong ingredients.

16. #14th Scariest Team down 1

A one-spot shuffle, nothing more. Paul's crew takes care of the ball and shoots it well — now it just needs the results to show up with dessert.

17. Willy the baller down 1

A gentle one-spot step for William, sitting on a tidy 3-2. Scoring and assists are the strong suit; the free throws are the one dish still in the oven.

18. Isaac Destroyer of Worlds even

Holding steady at the end of the table, and the table's always got room. Isaac dropped Obi Toppin, Jaylen Brown went to IL, but LaVine and Ryan Rollins are off the out list. Steals and threes give this roster a real base to build on.

That's the spread for Week 5. Whether you're feasting at the top or scraping the casserole dish at the bottom, remember: there's a whole lot of season left and the leftovers are always better the next day. Set your lineups before you set the table, be gracious in victory, be funny in defeat, and go easy on each other in the group chat. Gobble gobble, see you next week.
