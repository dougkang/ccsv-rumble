Week 4: Rise and Shine, Pass and Dish

Week 4 is in the books, and the top of the board got a little shuffle-y. A few teams stepped up, a few teams had the kind of week where the training room had more traffic than the box score, and most everybody else just quietly went about their business. Grab your coffee, scroll with your thumb, and remember: we're all one good Tuesday away from a glow-up.
